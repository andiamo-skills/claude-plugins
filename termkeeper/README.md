# Termkeeper

Termkeeper is for anyone who reads, studies or works in another language (most often English) but understands explanations better in their native language. Share slides, a PDF, a contract, a report, a manual or an article and ask about it in your own language: Claude answers in that language, while keeping technical terms, model names, acronyms and key words in the material's language, so you learn the concepts with the words you will actually need in exams, meetings and at work.

You don't need to ask for a language: writing in one language about material in another is enough. It works for any material language, not only English: a Spanish course keeps its Spanish terms.

## What it does
- Explains in your language, keeping key terms in the material's language (bold the first time, with a short translation only when the meaning isn't obvious).
- Adds a short glossary (max 10 terms) at the end of full explanations.
- Keeps answers to quick questions short: 2-4 lines, no glossary.
- Quiz and flashcard answers use the original terms.

## Examples
- Upload a contract in English and ask in Italian "cosa dice questa clausola?": Claude explains in Italian, keeping **indemnification** and **liability cap** in English.
- Ask "Explica esta diapositiva sobre el WACC" about English slides: Claude explains in Spanish, writing **WACC**, **cost of equity** and **capital structure** as they appear in the slides.

## Install
From Claude's plugin directory: search "Termkeeper".
