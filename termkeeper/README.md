# Termkeeper

Termkeeper is for people who study or work in English but understand explanations better in their native language. When you ask Claude to explain slides, PDFs, readings or cases written in English, it answers in the language you write in, while keeping technical terms, model names, acronyms and exam keywords in English, so you learn the concepts with the words you will actually need.

## What it does
- Explains in your language, keeping key terms in English (bold the first time, with a short translation only when the meaning isn't obvious).
- Adds a short glossary (max 10 terms) at the end of full explanations.
- Keeps answers to quick questions short: 2-4 lines, no glossary.
- Quiz and flashcard answers use the English terms.

## Example
Ask: "Explain this slide on WACC" in Italian, Spanish or any language. Claude explains in that language, writing **WACC**, **cost of equity** and **capital structure** in English.

## Privacy
This plugin contains one skill (plain instructions). It runs no code, sends no data anywhere and stores nothing.
